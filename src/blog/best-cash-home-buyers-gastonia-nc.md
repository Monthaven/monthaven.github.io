## The real distinction: buyers vs. lead brokers

Before names, understand the split. Two different kinds of business advertise identically:

**Actual buyers** purchase the house with their own money and take title. They have a
reason to be careful about condition, because they own the problem afterward.

**Lead brokers** collect your address and phone number and sell it, often to several
buyers at once. This is why you fill out one form and then get eleven calls. They have
no reason to care whether any of those buyers is good, only that the lead sold.

Both show you a form that says "get your cash offer." One of the two is not buying anything.

**How to check:** ask directly whether they are buying the house themselves. Then verify.
Property transfers in Gaston County are public record at the Register of Deeds. A company
that has bought thirty houses here can be looked up. A company that has bought none cannot.

## The four questions that expose a bad buyer

Ask every company on your list. The answers sort them quickly.

1. **"Are you buying this yourself, or selling my information?"** Hesitation is the answer.
2. **"Will the price change after you walk through?"** If there is an inspection contingency,
   the answer is yes, whatever they say now. Get the no in writing.
3. **"Can you show me how you got to this number?"** After-repair value, repair estimate,
   carrying costs, margin. A buyer who will not show the math is hiding one of the four.
4. **"What happens if I say no?"** A good answer is "nothing." Pressure and offers that
   expire in hours are both tells.

## When you should not sell to any of us

Gastonia's median home value is around $255,000, homes have been selling at roughly 99% of
list price, and average days on market have run near three weeks with about 1.4 months of
inventory — a genuinely tight market.

That matters: **if your house is in reasonable condition, list it.** In a market moving that
fast, a retail sale will very likely net you more than any cash offer on this page, even
after a 6% commission. Any cash buyer who tells you otherwise about a clean, move-in-ready
Gastonia house is not being straight with you.

Cash offers make sense in Gastonia for a narrower set of houses:

- Homes a retail lender will not finance — structural movement, roof failure, active water
  damage, failed septic. Much of the pre-war mill housing around Loray and York Chester
  falls here.
- Houses inherited, full, and being managed from out of state.
- Pre-foreclosure situations with a sale date on the calendar.
- Tenant-occupied rentals you would have to turn or evict before listing.

If none of those describe you, call a Gaston County agent first. We would rather tell you
